philosophy

Nothing invented.

company

An instrument,
not a factory.

jobpilot automates the labor of the search — the finding, the shaping, the sending, the remembering — and refuses, by design, to automate the truth.

Every claim it makes on your behalf is one you have already earned. A human hand approves every send. That is the whole philosophy, and it is not negotiable.
